Why Padua

An Australian-onshore, family-rooted team.

We are 100% Australian owned and 100% onshore. Our roots are in Kiama on the New South Wales south coast, where we built the business from the ground up — and where a meaningful share of our team still works today.

We're founder-led, deliberately human, and we take the long view. Our job is to help advisers help more Australians, and the people who do that work — paraplanners, technologists, researchers, support — sit at the centre of everything.

You'll work alongside experienced advice professionals on real client outcomes, with the technology, support and SLAs to back you up.

EARS — how we work

Four values, lived daily.

These aren't posters on a wall. They show up in how we triage a brief, how we respond to an adviser at 4pm on a Friday, and how we treat each other on a tough day.

E

Empathy

Listen first. Understand the adviser, the client, and the context — before doing the work.

A

Agility

Move with you. Adapt to changing briefs, platforms and timelines without losing the thread.

R

Reliability

SLAs met. When we say a draft will be back on Wednesday, it's back on Wednesday.

S

Simplicity

Cut complexity. Strip the work back to what's clear, defensible and useful — every time.

Where you could work with us

Six teams. One mission.

We don't post a long list of specific roles — when something opens, we want to hear from people who'd be a fit anywhere in the business. Tell us where you'd like to land, and we'll get in touch when there's a match.

Stream

Paraplanning

Build statements of advice for advisers across the country. Strategy work, document craft and platform fluency — the heart of what we do.

Stream

Contract Paraplanning

Flexible, project-based paraplanning for experienced practitioners who want to choose their hours and work across multiple advice businesses.

Stream

Transition Management

End-to-end implementation work — moving books of business between platforms, products and licensees, with the care that level of change demands.

Stream

Technology

Engineering, product and AI work on the Padua Portal, WealthX, SteveAI and the rest of the platform that powers our advice services.

Stream

Research

Investment and product research that informs our paraplanning work and gives advisers the depth they need behind every recommendation.

Stream

Internship

A way in for students and recent graduates curious about financial advice, paraplanning or fintech. Real work, real mentors, real responsibility.

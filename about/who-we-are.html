Our story
We're Anne-Marie and Matthew Esler, founders of Padua — a company that, despite its Italian name, is 100% Australian-owned and operated. Named in tribute to our brother Anthony and the city of Padua in Italy, we're deeply rooted in family values, with our Head Office in Kiama, New South Wales.

We built Padua because we saw, first-hand, what advisers were carrying. The administrative weight. The compliance load. The hours that should have been spent with clients but were spent on paperwork instead.

So we built the company we wanted to work with. One that takes operational complexity off advisers' shoulders, with skilled people and intelligent technology working alongside them — not instead of them.

Anne-Marie & Matthew Esler · Co-founders, Padua Solutions
Our mission

Padua connects financial advisers with highly skilled human resources and intelligent, proprietary technology.

Our Why

Why we exist.

Demand for quality financial advice in Australia is rising. The supply of experienced advisers is not. Too many Australians who need advice cannot access it, and too many advisers who want to help more people are overwhelmed by the operational reality of the work.

We exist to close that gap. By giving advisers the operational leverage they need — skilled people, intelligent technology, and a partner who understands the work — we help them spend more time on what only they can do: the human conversations that define great advice.

The result: more advisers serving more Australians, with more confidence in the work behind every recommendation.

Padua How

How we deliver.

Padua operates as a single integrated platform that brings together three things advisers need to scale: experienced onshore advice professionals, proprietary workflow and AI technology, and end-to-end delivery from discovery to ongoing review.

Everything runs through the Padua Portal — the workflow layer that connects adviser, paraplanner, compliance and client. Each piece of work moves through a defined process, with SLAs, audit trails and quality checks built in.

One platform. Skilled people. Real workflow. Outcomes you can stake your reputation on.

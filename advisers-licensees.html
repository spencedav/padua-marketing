Our methodology

Every piece of advice follows four steps.

A consistent, repeatable methodology that runs through the portal, the paraplanners, and the partnership. It's how we keep quality the same on the first plan and the thousandth.

Step 01

Discover

Uncover the client's goals, risks and existing position. Data is gathered, verified, and consolidated in one place.

Step 02

Compare

Model strategies and product options side-by-side. Quantitative comparisons replace gut feel.

Step 03

Recommend

Produce the Statement of Advice. Strategy, products and projections come together in a defensible document.

Step 04

Review

Ongoing reviews and Records of Advice. Plans stay current as the client's life changes.

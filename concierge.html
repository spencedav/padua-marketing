Concierge is the managed-service way to run your next platform or investment transition.

You don't have to project-manage this. We do.

Concierge combines our Transition Management Program technology with a dedicated onshore team who own the program from comparison through to client implementation. We segment your book, build the packs, manage the SOAs and applications, and push the documentation back into your CRM.

It's built for advice firms and licensees running platform or investment transitions at scale — where the operational complexity would otherwise pull principals and senior advisers off client work for months.

The Concierge Program

On your mark. Set. Go.

Three stages, run for you by the same team. Each stage hands cleanly to the next so nothing falls between people, systems or providers.

Stage 1

On your mark

Fee Comparison

We provide advice firms with a platform fee comparison ($ & %) of their client's existing platforms and investments against the recommended scenario. This helps the advice firm identify potential cost and benefit enhancements from moving platform or investment strategies and make informed decisions with clear data.

1 Cost & benefit clarity, client by client
Stage 2

Set

Engagement

We segment the advice firm's client base and get you set for client engagement. We've developed an advice request system that knows when implementation packs are required. We segment the advice firm's clients by costs, benefits, platform, adviser, FUA, advice type, number of products and much more.

2 The right client, the right pack, the right time
Stage 3

Go

Implementation

We produce the implementation pack for each advice firm client, including SOA, application and other transition documentation. We integrate with the advice firm's CRM to push back into the client folder.

3 Packs delivered, CRM in sync

The Concierge dashboard

Track progress in real time.

You stay in the loop without running the program. The Concierge dashboard gives principals and licensees a live view of every client in flight — what stage they're at, what pack is due, what's been signed, and where the next bottleneck is.

  • Live status across every client in the transition
  • Stage-by-stage view: comparison, engagement, implementation
  • Segmentation by adviser, platform, FUA, advice type, product count
  • Implementation packs and CRM activity in one place

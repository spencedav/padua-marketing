Sunrise, Kiama

At Padua Solutions, our purpose is simple. Empowering advisers to build stronger futures for more Australians.

Demand for financial advice is rising. The supply of experienced advisers is constrained. Too many Australians who need quality advice cannot access it, and too many advisers who want to help more people find themselves overwhelmed by administration, compliance and operational complexity.

We don't replace advisers. We empower them. We take on the operational weight that surrounds advice, so advisers can spend more time on relationships, strategy and the human work that defines great advice.

About Padua

Helping advisers do their best work, so more Australians can access quality advice.

We are 100% onshore, Australian owned, and ISO 27001 certified. Your client data never leaves Australia.

Our team combines experienced advice professionals, intelligent workflow and AI to give advisers the operational leverage they need.

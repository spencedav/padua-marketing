Padua WealthX

Secure open-banking that gives clients clear insights into their income, expenses, assets and liabilities — and generates referral opportunities back to advice firms. The fastest way to build a complete client financial picture.

  • Secure open-banking connection Bank-grade, consent-based data sharing. Clients link their accounts in minutes.
  • Income & expense insights Categorised cashflow with the patterns and trends you need to assess serviceability.
  • Assets & liabilities at a glance A full position summary pulled directly from the source — no manual data entry.
  • Referral opportunities to advice firms Surface clients who would benefit from financial advice and route them to a partner firm.
  • Faster client onboarding Replace stacks of statements with a single, accurate financial snapshot.
  • 100% Australian, ISO 27001 certified Client data is handled onshore by an ISO 27001 certified team. It never leaves Australia.

The Padua methodology

Discover. Compare. Recommend. Review.

The same four-phase methodology that underpins quality advice is how we partner with product providers — applied to your transitions, your integrations and your distribution into the advice market.

01 · Discover
Understand your business.

We start with your platform, your members, your funds and your operating model — not a generic vendor playbook. Our teams embed to map what good looks like for you.

02 · Compare
Model the options.

We benchmark the technology, integration paths and transition approaches that fit your shape — then show the trade-offs in language your stakeholders can act on.

03 · Recommend
Build the path.

We deliver the recommended solution — transition program, integration build, data product or distribution channel — onshore, on time, and SLA-backed.

04 · Review
Keep it working.

We stay with the work after launch. Ongoing service, data insights and program reviews keep what we deliver aligned to your evolving business.

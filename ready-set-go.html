The methodology

A four-stage operating model for platform and investment transitions — built around your client base, not ours.

Ready, Set, Go is how we manage the work that happens between deciding to transition and pressing send on the implementation pack. Four stages — On your mark, Ready, Set and Go — that move advice firms from a fee comparison through to fully implemented client outcomes, with our team project-managing every step and our technology doing the heavy lifting in between.

It's the same program we run for licensee-wide transitions, individual book transfers, and platform consolidations — the difference is the scale, not the method.

01 Discover

On your mark.Fee Comparison

We provide advice firms with a platform fee comparison ($ & %) of their client's existing platforms & investments against the recommended scenario. This helps the advice firm identify potential cost & benefit enhancements from moving platform or investment strategies and make informed decisions with clear data.

Side-by-side comparison of existing vs. recommended platform & investments — in dollars and percentage points.
Surfaces cost and benefit enhancements before any client conversation begins.
Clear data to support informed decision-making by the advice firm and licensee.
Start with a fee comparison
02 Compare

Ready.Analysis

We help the advice firm understand their client advice data, including potential CRM & portfolio information gaps. We provide advice firms with a detailed analysis of each of their client's accounts — the existing platform & investments against any recommended scenario, highlighting the specific tax & other impacts of transitioning. Advice firms will also receive insights into the platform or investment strategy transition effectiveness, summarised in interactive charts and graphs.

Per-account analysis of existing platform & investments against the recommended scenario.
Surfaces CRM and portfolio data gaps before they become advice risk.
Tax and other transition impacts modelled at the client level.
Transition effectiveness summarised in interactive charts and graphs.
Request the analysis
03 Recommend

Set.Engagement

We segment the advice firm's client base and get you set for client engagement. We've developed an advice request system that knows when implementation packs are required. We segment the advice firm's clients by costs, benefits, platform, adviser, FUA, advice type, number of products and much more.

Client base segmented by cost, benefit, platform, adviser, FUA, advice type, number of products and more.
Advice request system that knows when an implementation pack is required.
Engagement sequencing matched to the firm's adviser capacity and client priorities.
Plan client engagement
04 Review

Go.Implementation

We produce the implementation pack for each advice firm client, including SOA, application & other transition documentation. We integrate with the advice firm's CRM to push back into the client folder or file where available. Monitor progress with a user-friendly, dashboard-style view.

Production of each client's implementation pack — SOA, application and transition documentation.
CRM integration pushes documentation back to the client folder or file where available.
Progress monitored through a user-friendly, dashboard-style view.
